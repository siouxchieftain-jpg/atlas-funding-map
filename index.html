HOW TO READ

Fit

  • Open nowCan apply as things stand.
  • After a stepOpens once something is in place: a company, a partner or the finished PhD.
  • Via a partnerA hospital, municipality or university applies, with ATLAS Connect in the project.
  • DependsTurns on personal details such as citizenship or residence.
  • Not a fitKept so it is not looked up again.
HOW TO READ

Priority

  • HighOpen now or within about a year, or a partner route is clear.
  • MediumOne to two years away, a partner route still unclear, or an investor.
  • LowClosed or not a fit but relevant, more than two years off, or a service, event or prize.
